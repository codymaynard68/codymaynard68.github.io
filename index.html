Bookkeeper Gmail Read Only

Bookkeeper Gmail Read Only is a private, personal-use tool operated by Cody Maynard. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

The tool runs only on the owner's own Windows computer. With the owner's permission, it uses Google's read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) to find receipt emails that the owner and his staff send to his own mailbox, and saves copies of the attached receipts locally so they can be matched to his businesses' bookkeeping records.

It cannot send, delete, label, or change email. It does not use any other Google service.

See the Privacy Policy for how Google user data is handled.
