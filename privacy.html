Privacy Policy

App: Bookkeeper Gmail Read Only  ·  Operator: Cody Maynard  ·  Effective: November 2, 2026

Who uses this app

This is a private, personal-use application. The only Google account that authorizes it is the operator's own account.

Google user data accessed

The app requests only the Gmail read-only scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). It searches for receipt messages and reads those messages and their attachments.

How the data is used

Receipt messages and attachments are used solely to record and document the operator's own business bookkeeping. The app does not send, modify, delete, or label email.

Storage and security

The app runs only on the operator's computer. The Google authorization is stored encrypted on that computer using Windows data protection. Saved receipt copies stay in the operator's local bookkeeping files.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Receipt records may be entered into the operator's own accounting software for his businesses.

Limited Use

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local copies can be deleted by the operator.
