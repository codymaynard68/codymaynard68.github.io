Privacy Policy

App: cpAI (QuickBooks Online read-only bookkeeping assistant)  ·  Operator: Cody Maynard  ·  Effective: November 6, 2026

Who uses this app

This is a private, internal application. It is not offered to the public or listed in any app marketplace. It is connected only to QuickBooks Online companies owned or managed by the operator: Texoma Counseling & Development, Phoenix Heritage Holdings, Inc., and Phoenix Behavioral Health & Wellness, LLC.

QuickBooks data accessed

The app requests the QuickBooks Online Accounting scope (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting). It reads company information, accounting records (such as accounts, transactions, vendors and customers) and financial reports. The app's own software is read-only: it does not create, change or delete QuickBooks records.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to review, reconcile and report on the operator's own business bookkeeping, for example checking for duplicate entries and preparing month-end reports.

Storage and security

The app runs only on the operator's own computer. The QuickBooks authorization and app credentials are stored encrypted on that computer using Windows data protection. Data retrieved from QuickBooks stays in the operator's local bookkeeping files.

Sharing

QuickBooks data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. It is processed by the operator's own AI bookkeeping assistant only to perform the bookkeeping tasks described above.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps), or by the operator deleting the local encrypted authorization files.
